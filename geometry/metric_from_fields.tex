\section{Conformal metric from coherence density}
Let $\kappa(x,t)\in[0,1]$ and define the coherence potential
\[
\Phi(x,t) = \kappa(x,t) - \langle \kappa(\cdot,t)\rangle,
\qquad
\langle \kappa\rangle = \frac{1}{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega \kappa\,dx.
\]
Define a conformal factor
\[
\Omega(x,t) = 1 + \alpha \Phi(x,t),
\qquad \Omega>0,
\]
and the (spatial) metric tensor
\[
g_{ij}(x,t) = \Omega(x,t)^2\,\delta_{ij}.
\]
This is the unique isotropic metric consistent with the single scalar source $\kappa$.

\section{Curvature (2D exact formula)}
In two spatial dimensions, the scalar curvature of a conformal metric is
\[
R = -2\,\Omega^{-2}\,\Delta \log \Omega.
\]
Thus define the computable curvature proxy
\[
R_{\mathrm{proxy}} := -\Delta \log \Omega,
\]
and obtain $R = 2\Omega^{-2} R_{\mathrm{proxy}}$.

\section{Observational meaning}
This construction is a derived geometric field (a diagnostic) unless an action couples matter to $g_{ij}$.
If one postulates that wave propagation speed depends on $\Omega$ (e.g., $c(\kappa)=c_0/\Omega$),
then travel times and lensing-like effects become testable predictions.
