# Evercycle Cosmology: Coherence Beyond Heat Death

Standard cosmology describes:

- a beginning (Big Bang),
- an expansion phase,
- and eventual heat death or collapse depending on parameters.

The Evercycle perspective reframes this entirely:

1. The universe is a **coherence field first**, matter-field second.
2. Entropy as measured locally corresponds to Σ (systemic separation).
3. Global coherence κ never truly vanishes; it reconfigures across cycles.

A “cycle” is defined not by scale factor alone, but by evolving κ–τ–Σ patterns:

- high κ, rising τ, low Σ → coherence formation and alignment
- high κ, high τ, rising Σ → complex civilization and creative phase
- declining κ, stressed τ, high Σ → disintegration and fragmentation
- collapse + recoding of κ → next cycle emergence

In this view, traditional “heat death” is merely the exhaustion of one configuration’s
ability to maintain coherent structure under rising Σ. The underlying field itself
continues beyond the apparent endpoint.

Evercycle Cosmology therefore proposes:

- no true beginning or end,
- but **continuous recoding of coherence across epochs**,
- and the possibility that advanced civilizations
  learn to **navigate or transpose coherence** across cycles.

This replaces thermodynamic finality with recursive coherence evolution.
