# Comparison to Known Models

| Model | Similarity | Difference |
|-------|------------|------------|
| Fisher–KPP | Logistic growth + diffusion | Adds τ gating and Σ suppression |
| Gross–Pitaevskii | Nonlinear amplitude | No complex phase here |
| Phase Field | Order parameter diffusion | Adds temporal responsibility field |
| Decoherence models | Entropy coupling | Explicit Σ field with feedback |

The κ equation reduces to Fisher–KPP when τ=1 and Σ=0.
